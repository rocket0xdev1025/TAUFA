How it works, in four lines

  1. Plant a sigil on a lit plot. Each sale lifts the price 0.3%; the part above 0.03 ETH halves every 6 hours.
  2. 45% of every later sigil is split evenly among the sigils standing. TAUFA trading fees fill a pool that pays them about 7.5% a day.
  3. Rams crack sigils; posts stop rams. A cracked sigil stops earning and falls at the next bell unless repaired.
  4. After the siege weekend, 90% of the war chest is paid to what still stands. Fallen sigils keep half a share.
